Official Statement

A commitment to serve

Public service begins with a simple belief: that every person has a role to play in building a better country. As young people, we cannot wait for the future to be handed to us — we must step forward, serve our communities, and take responsibility for the Seychelles we want to inherit.

This office exists to serve, to listen, to support youth, and to build a stronger connection between young people and the future of our nation. A brighter Seychelles will not be built by one person or one generation alone. It will be built by all of us.

Tyler P. NicholasFounder, Office of Tyler Nicholas
