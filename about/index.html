The Foundation

The Tyler Nicholas Foundation

The Tyler Nicholas Foundation is the philanthropic arm of Tyler's mission — dedicated to empowering young people, supporting education, and strengthening communities across Seychelles.

Through its programmes and partnerships, the Foundation turns ideas into real opportunities for the next generation, ensuring more young Seychellois have the tools, support and confidence to contribute to their country.

National Service

Honourable Member — Seychelles National Youth Assembly

As an Honourable Member of the Seychelles National Youth Assembly, Tyler represents the voices of young Seychellois at a national level.

The Assembly gives young people a platform to debate, propose and contribute to the discussions that shape the country's future — ensuring the next generation has a genuine say in the Seychelles it will inherit. For Tyler, it is a responsibility to listen carefully, represent faithfully, and speak up for the young people he serves.

International Engagement

Youth Ambassador

Tyler represented Seychelles as a Youth Ambassador through the Youth Ambassadors programme — a U.S. Department of State exchange, implemented by PH International, that brings together young leaders from across Africa to build skills in civic engagement and leadership.

Centred on civic engagement, the exchange combines leadership seminars, service learning and cultural immersion across the United States. On returning home, each participant designs and delivers a Community Action Plan that addresses a real need — turning the experience into lasting impact.

My Country

For Seychelles

Seychelles is more than where Tyler comes from — it is the reason behind everything he does. A young island nation scattered across the Indian Ocean, Seychelles is home to some of the most beautiful places on earth: granite peaks and turquoise water, quiet coves like Anse Royale, and forests found nowhere else in the world.

But its greatest strength has always been its people — proud, warm, resilient and diverse, drawn from many cultures into one nation. In just a few decades, Seychelles has grown from a small colony into an independent country with its own voice, its own identity, and a standard of living that is the envy of the region.

Tyler believes the story is far from finished. He sees a country with extraordinary potential — one that can lead in education, embrace technology and innovation, protect its environment, and stand confidently on the world stage through diplomacy and cooperation rather than division.

He believes deeply that the future of Seychelles belongs to its young people, and that they must be trusted, educated and empowered to build it. Every role he takes, every project he starts and every ambition he holds returns to a single purpose: to help build a stronger, more peaceful and more prosperous Seychelles for the generations still to come.
