The Foundation

The Tyler Nicholas Foundation

The Tyler Nicholas Foundation is the philanthropic arm of Tyler's mission — dedicated to empowering young people, supporting education, and strengthening communities across Seychelles.

Through its programmes and partnerships, the Foundation works to turn ideas into real opportunities for the next generation, ensuring that more young Seychellois have the tools, support and confidence to contribute to their country.

National Service

Honourable Member — Seychelles National Youth Assembly

As an Honourable Member of the Seychelles National Youth Assembly, Tyler represents the voices of young Seychellois at a national level.

The Assembly gives young people a platform to debate, propose and contribute to the discussions that shape the country's future — ensuring the next generation has a genuine say in the Seychelles it will inherit. For Tyler, it is a responsibility to listen carefully, represent faithfully, and speak up for the young people he serves.

Preparing leaders of tomorrow

The Youth Ambassadors Program strengthens mutual understanding, prosperity and global collaboration by empowering youth leaders to become active, responsible citizens both locally and globally.

The program fosters mutual respect — helping to share values, build relationships and bridge cultural divides to promote peaceful coexistence. By developing critical thinking, problem-solving and civic-action skills, participants return home equipped to contribute to stability and progress within their own nations.

The exchange experience

The overall theme of the program is civic engagement. The exchange brings together highly selected participants to promote mutual understanding, increase leadership capacity, and prepare youth to implement tangible change within their local communities.

It builds team spirit through hands-on service learning, leadership seminars, peer collaboration, and visits to entrepreneurial ventures and innovative organisations — from the policy-focused landscape of Washington, D.C. to the grassroots civil-rights history of the American South.

Community action & impact

Civic education and youth leadership build participants' self-confidence and capacity to drive solutions to challenges back home. A central objective is the Action Planning Academy, where participants develop a concrete framework for execution.

Upon returning to Seychelles, participants apply what they have learned by formally implementing a Community Action Plan — a Community Impact Project that serves critical needs and ensures long-term civic progress.

My Country

For Seychelles

Seychelles is more than where Tyler comes from — it is the reason behind everything he does. A young island nation in the heart of the Indian Ocean, Seychelles is home to extraordinary natural beauty, a proud and diverse people, and enormous potential.

Tyler believes deeply in its future: that through education, innovation, unity and responsible leadership, Seychelles can stand confident and prosperous on the world stage. Every project, every role and every ambition comes back to one goal — to help build a stronger, brighter Seychelles.
